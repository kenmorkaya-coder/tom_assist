## Independent semantic checks

These checks are fixed before repaired output is observed. The compiler must
report supported/unsupported status explicitly; passing a bounded battery is
not a universal exactness guarantee.

1. Strict versus inclusive comparisons: `above 5` versus `at least 5` are
   different; `below 5` versus `at most 5` are different.
2. Equivalent units: `1 m` and `1000 mm` are the same physical quantity;
   `1 m` and `1000 m` are different. Normalize using exact decimal/rational
   arithmetic with a dimensioned unit identity, not binary rounding or fuzzy
   unit-name similarity.
3. Numerical representation: `600 kg`, `600.0 kg` and `six hundred kilograms`
   should agree where supported; `600 kg` and `900 kg` must remain distinct.
4. Signs and bounds: negative numbers, zero and range endpoints must not be
   dropped or misread as punctuation. Unsupported ranges must be explicit.
5. Scope: two thresholds in one sentence must remain attached to their own
   measured quantities and actions. Swapping thresholds must not preserve the
   same record merely because the unordered value set is unchanged.
6. Conditions: `only if`, `unless`, `even if` and `without` must not be reduced
   to a shared keyword list. Preserve the exact evidence span and its link to
   the governed action. Missing/ambiguous scope must not trigger a whole-sentence
   fallback disguised as an exact condition.
7. Polarity: prohibition is not equivalent to a negated measured quantity or a
   negated condition. Surface `not` counting does not establish logical scope.
8. Identity: exact document/asset/revision identifiers are not interchangeable
   with their semantic-family similarity. Dates and IDs were not demonstrated
   failure categories in the original battery; report any new checks separately.

## Limitations and follow-up boundary

- This is not wired into production. No Tree, memory, provider, OAuth, live
  migration, experience or temporal-authority operation was performed.
- Overall result remains RED. Do not advance to temporal-authority testing.
- Typed symbols are deterministic identity codes, not an ordering-preserving
  number system in cosine space or an unlimited collision-free representation.
- Dates, revision/asset IDs, arbitrary ranges and multiple quantity/condition
  scopes are explicitly unsupported by this v1 adapter. They must not be
  represented as solved by the quantity/unit checks.
- Quote binding does not verify model-inferred relations. The experiment used
  manually supplied events and did not test event extraction by GPT or Gemma.
- One original quantity paraphrase says `keep ... at forty litres per second`,
  whereas the canonical says `limit ... to 40 litres per second`. The adapter
  records EQ versus LE respectively. Its distance-margin ratio is 1.0988, below
  1.25, although the aggregate original gate allows this one margin miss. The
  original statements, labels and threshold were preserved; this semantic
  annotation concern is disclosed rather than silently repaired.
- No further repair version was attempted after inspecting the scores. Future
  changes require a new version and fresh holdout, not retuning this run.
