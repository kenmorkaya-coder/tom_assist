## Result

The prior implementation did not comply with three important boundaries:

1. Retrieved document excerpts appeared inside `TOM_ASSIST_STATE`, under an
   instruction calling that state authoritative.
2. Broad research searched authored units throughout the document inventory,
   even where the question's selected Tree region had not retrieved a chunk.
   Having an ingestion receipt was being treated as equivalent to retrieval.
3. Evaluation could commit conversation experience automatically after PASS,
   or after findings were resolved, without the user's explicit acceptance.

The local corrections separate document evidence from authoritative state,
require a query-region Tree match before candidate scoring and authored-unit
research, and require persisted explicit acceptance before experience commit.
The acceptance correction covers both provider conversations and manual capture.
Existing committed history is preserved. Source documents and Tree mechanics
are not rewritten to force a desired retrieval result.

These are compliance corrections, **not proof that comprehensive deed research
works**. No new provider response was generated during this review.
